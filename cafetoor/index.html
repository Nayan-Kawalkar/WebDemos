(01)The House

Cafetoor is a neighbourhood café with a garden heart — bright pressed juices, patient espresso, warm bakes, and the kind of welcome that makes a first visit feel familiar.

Come for the coffee. Stay for the light, the greenery, and one more slice for the table.

  1. 01

    Coffee

    Pulled slowly and poured with care — espresso, pour over and a long, cold brew.

  2. 02

    Juice

    Pressed to order from whole fruit and greens, bright and cold from the bar.

  3. 03

    Bakery

    Croissants, loaves and cakes, baked here every morning for the counter.
